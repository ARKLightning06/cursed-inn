# cursed-inn
Welcome! This is a repo for 2025-2026 Winter Game Jam at UofT, with the theme of Recursion.

## The Game
You can [play The Cursed Inn](https://arklightning06.itch.io/the-cursed-inn) on itch.io!

## About
This game was created on a team of 5, 4 programmers and 1 artist, working together for the 2025-2026 Winter Game Jam with University of Toronto's GDDC (Game Design and Development Club). Thanks to artist Phoebe Lewis and programmers Andrew Koes, Andrew Li, Ali Khan, and Yeonji Lee! 

The theme for the game jam was recursion. We integrated recursion in two main ways: first was with the theming, as the gameplay mirrors recursion by trapping the innkeeper in an timeloop they must escape from. The aim of the game is to discover how they were trapped in this loop, and to escape it by deducing who the werewolf/sorcerer/sorceress who cursed them is. The second way is through humor (or maybe lack of humor). After finishing the game, the player will receive the message:

"Coming soon, the sequel: Re-cursed inn!"

Haha, hilarious, so funny. Apologies. It served as an inspiration for theming, and if you have a sufficiently developed or maybe underdeveloped sense of humor maybe it's good for a laugh.

Anyway, that's the framework behind the game!

## Gameplay
The Cursed Inn was conceived as primarily a story centered game. The player will move around their inn, visiting 8 different NPCs and learning about their stories, learning about what happened and solving the mystery of the curse. There are also several simple puzzles to solve and items to use. 

## Limitations
The GDDC game jam allowed for two months of development. With the busy schedules of a UofT student, combined with several issues including an unfortunately broken wrist of our sole artist, we had to strategically adjust expectations and downsize from the original plans. As of writing this README, the first half of the Cursed Inn is finished and the second half is still in development.
